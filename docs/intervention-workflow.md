# Closed-Loop Intervention Lifecycle & Workflow

## 1. Concept & Rationale
In high-stakes human support, automated systems must never operate as a "fire-and-forget" alert mechanism. Sahara implements a **Closed-Loop Intervention Lifecycle** that tracks every support action from recommendation to verified outcome.

---

## 2. Standard Intervention Lifecycle States
Every intervention follows a strictly enforced transition graph:

1. `RECOMMENDED`: Initial recommendation generated by assessment heuristic.
2. `ACCEPTED`: Counsellor reviews and validates the appropriateness of the action.
3. `ASSIGNED`: Formally assigned to a specific welfare officer or support team.
4. `CONTACT_ATTEMPTED`: Outreach initiated (call made, visit attempted).
5. `CONTACTED`: Direct two-way contact established with victim/family.
6. `IN_PROGRESS`: Support plan actively being delivered (legal aid, therapy, financial relief).
7. `COMPLETED`: Intervention successfully carried out.
8. `CLOSED`: Final case audit and resolution recorded.

```
RECOMMENDED ────► ACCEPTED ────► ASSIGNED ────► CONTACT_ATTEMPTED
     │                                                │
     ▼                                                ▼
  CLOSED ◄──────────────────────────────────────── CONTACTED
     ▲                                                │
     │                                                ▼
  CLOSED ◄────────── COMPLETED ◄──────────────── IN_PROGRESS
```

---

## 3. Documented Outcome Categories
When an intervention reaches terminal state (`COMPLETED` or `CLOSED`), the counsellor records a structured outcome:
- `stabilized`: Victim distress reduced to manageable level.
- `resolved`: Immediate distress driver successfully remediated (e.g. police protection posted).
- `declined`: Victim voluntarily elected not to take up offered service.
- `transferred`: Case transferred to specialised agency (e.g. Child Welfare Committee, NALSA).
- `unreachable`: Exhausted outreach attempts; referred to field welfare check.

---

## 4. Counsellor Triage Queues
The Counsellor Desk categorizes cases into dynamic operational queues:
- **New**: Unreviewed cases with new check-in data.
- **Under Review**: Cases currently undergoing assessment.
- **Contacted**: Cases where victim has responded and initial contact is confirmed.
- **Assigned**: Active interventions assigned to a welfare officer.
- **Follow-up Due**: Scheduled re-assessment window reached.
- **Resolved**: Completed cases archived for longitudinal monitoring.
