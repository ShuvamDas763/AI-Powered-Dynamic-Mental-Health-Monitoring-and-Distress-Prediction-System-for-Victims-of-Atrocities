# Future Clinical & Institutional Validation Roadmap

## 1. Non-Claims & Current Status
- **Current Prototype Status**: Sahara is a technological and algorithmic demonstration developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26094).
- **Synthetic Data**: All personas, check-in transcripts, and records in this repository are **100% synthetic**. No real victim records or protected personal identifiers exist in this codebase.
- **Non-Clinical Disclaimer**: This software is **NOT** a certified medical device, psychiatric diagnostic system, or autonomous government adjudication tool. It must never replace human clinical judgement or statutory legal procedures.

---

## 2. Institutional Validation Roadmap
Before real-world deployment in state welfare departments or District Legal Services Authorities (DLSAs), the following phases must be executed:

### Phase 1: Ethical & Institutional Review Board (IRB) Clearance
- Comprehensive review by an accredited Human Subjects Ethics Committee.
- Protocol for handling severe vicarious trauma and secondary stress among counsellors.
- Alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023).

### Phase 2: Inter-Rater Reliability & Clinical Calibration
- Double-blind evaluation comparing the empirical early-warning heuristic with gold-standard assessments (e.g. PHQ-9, PCL-5, trauma screening inventories) conducted by licensed clinical psychologists.
- Calibration of the escalation threshold ($T = 65$) across diverse sociolinguistic cohorts.

### Phase 3: Field Usability & Legal Harmonization
- Pilot study with District Welfare Officers and legal aid advocates under the auspices of National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment.
- Evaluation of accessibility across rural vernacular speakers and low-literacy complainants.
