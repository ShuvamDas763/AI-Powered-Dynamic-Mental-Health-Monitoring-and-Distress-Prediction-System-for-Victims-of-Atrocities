1. Executive Summary & Problem Engineering

1.1 The Missing Psychological Layer in Legal Redressal

The National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA - 14566) provides institutional mechanisms to lodge grievances, register FIRs, and track legal progress for atrocities against Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. However, criminal proceedings under the SC/ST (PoA) Act take an average of 18 to 36+ months across Special Courts. During this protracted window:

  • Witness Intimidation: Accused parties apply extra-legal pressure, social boycott, and overt threats to turn witnesses hostile.
  • Procedural Exhaustion: Repeated adjournments and institutional fatigue drain victim morale, driving severe clinical distress.
  • The Silent Failure Mode: Victims rarely file formal complaints about distress until they abruptly stop attending court dates, withdraw cases, or succumb to self-harm.

Sahara (सहारा) implements the missing longitudinal mental health monitoring and distress prediction layer. It functions as an objective, trauma-informed triage platform for designated Welfare Officers, District Magistrates, and DLSA legal teams.

1.2 Core Architectural Axioms

Axiom Engineering Choice Rationale
1. Determinism over Hallucination Distress scores and escalation flags are computed via deterministic arithmetic, never by an LLM prompt. Pre-build probes proved LLMs are non-deterministic: identical transcripts score 45 on Monday and 55 on Tuesday. A judicial support system requires reproducible arithmetic.
2. Non-Clinical Stance No clinical or psychiatric diagnoses (e.g. PTSD, major depression) are generated. The system evaluates support indicators and observable behavior, preserving ethical boundaries and clinician autonomy.
3. Two-Tier Privacy Barrier Complete decoupling of Identified Case Data (Tier 1) from Administrative Aggregates (Tier 2). Compliance with Section 8 of DPDP Act 2023 and SC/ST Act identity protection mandates. Zero PII ever crosses to Tier 2.
4. Dual-Route Safety Gate Hybrid crisis detection combining a sub-millisecond local pattern engine with deep semantic LLM analysis. Ensures zero-latency protection offline while capturing novel metaphors and colloquial expressions of despair.

2. Mathematical Formulation: The Composite Scoring Engine

2.1 Why Not an End-to-End LLM?

Jury Challenge: "Why not prompt Claude or GPT-4 to read the chat and output a score from 1 to 100?"
Technical Answer: LLMs are autoregressive token predictors with zero calibrated certainty guarantees. In empirical calibration probes (scripts/verify-model-safety.js), feeding identical intimidation check-ins to frontier models yielded scores ranging from 45 to 55, and both models failed to escalate a high-risk witness threat because they treated polite, subdued language as "low concern." Allowing an LLM to generate the final numeric score introduces unexplainable variance, susceptibility to prompt injection, and catastrophic false negatives.

2.2 The 4-Component Blend Formula

In Sahara, three-quarters of the distress score is computed directly from verifiable behavioral records. The LLM produces exactly one bounded variable (surfaceSentiment), which is weighted strictly below 50% so it can never overrule objective longitudinal evidence.

Distress Score S = (0.45 · S_sentiment) + (0.35 · D_disengagement) + (0.12 · Δ_trend) + (0.08 · B_pattern)
Component Weight Source Mathematical Definition & Behavioral Meaning
S_sentiment 45% LLM reading Bounded integer $[0, 100]$ extracted by Groq inference. Quantifies the emotional distress visible in the current message text alone.
D_disengagement 35% Deterministic Arithmetic Measures word count collapse relative to the person's historical baseline:
D = 100 · (1 - min(1, W_current / W_baseline))
If a victim who previously wrote 80 words collapses to "fine" (1 word), $D \to 100$.
Δ_trend 12% Deterministic Arithmetic Linear regression slope over rolling check-in window ($k \ge 3$):
slope = Cov(t, S) / Var(t). Normalized to $[0, 100]$. Measures acceleration of deterioration.
B_pattern 8% Deterministic Rule-Matching Boost points triggered by explicit statutory risk markers: witness intimidation (+25), social boycott (+20), or repeated trial postponements (+15).

2.3 Mathematical Proof of Monotonicity

A fundamental requirement of legal and clinical triage systems is monotonicity: raising any risk input must never decrease the resulting composite score.

∂S / ∂S_sentiment = 0.45 > 0,    ∂S / ∂D_disengagement = 0.35 > 0,    ∂S / ∂Δ_trend = 0.12 > 0,    ∂S / ∂B_pattern = 0.08 > 0

Because all partial derivatives are strictly positive constants, the system guarantees strict monotonicity: no single polite sentence (e.g. "I'm fine 🙃") can drag an escalating case into the safe band if participation has collapsed.

2.4 Risk Band Mapping

Scores are mapped into four discrete, citable triage bands (server/src/domain/distressScore.js):

  • LOW (0 - 30): Routine longitudinal monitoring. Next check-in in 14 days.
  • MODERATE (31 - 49): Subtle strain or process fatigue detected. Check-in interval tightened to 7 days.
  • ELEVATED (50 - 69): Significant distress or participation drop. Case review flagged for next welfare cycle.
  • HIGH (70 - 100): Acute distress or compounded risk. Mandatory human welfare officer intervention within 24 hours.

3. Dual-Route (Hybrid) Crisis Architecture

3.1 The Failure Mode of Static Regex vs Pure LLMs

During empirical stress-testing, human subjects expressing acute suicidal thoughts used colloquial phrasing ("i just wanna kill myself"). Static regex libraries looking for rigid formal syntax (/i want to kill myself/) failed to trigger because of the interjection "just" and slang "wanna". Conversely, pure LLMs without strict deterministic gates risk dropping alerts under network latency or prompt injection.

Sahara implements a Dual-Route Safety Net where either route is individually sufficient to trigger the maximum emergency protocol.

CrisisTriggered = Route_1(Regex Engine)  ∨  Route_2(Semantic LLM Intent)  ∨  (Sentiment ≥ 85 ∧ Hopelessness)
Property Route 1: Deterministic Pattern Engine Route 2: Deep Semantic AI Classifier
Mechanism Local compiled regex over token stream (crisisDetection.js). System prompt classifier evaluated by Groq (prompts.js).
Latency < 0.5 milliseconds (zero network overhead). ~250 - 450 milliseconds via Groq inference.
Offline Capability 100% active even if server network dies or during cached fallback. Requires active network connection.
Nuance Coverage Explicit methods, means, poisons, weapons (e.g. pills, noose, फाँसी). Metaphors, indirect hopelessness (e.g. "ready to turn off the lights forever").
Failure Mode Misses novel colloquialisms or poetic despair. Can suffer API timeout or rate limiting.
Synergy Complete coverage: Route 1 provides an unshakeable offline floor; Route 2 captures human emotional nuance.

3.2 Emergency Action Protocol & QPR Integration

When the Crisis Gate activates, the system executes an automated statutory sequence:

  1. Immediate UI Reconfiguration: The client check-in immediately renders the pinned Emergency SOS Banner with 1-tap dialer buttons for:
    • Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1-800-891-4416) — Ministry of Health & Family Welfare 24/7 mental health emergency helpline.
    • National Emergency Helpline (112) — Immediate physical safety intervention.
  2. Stage 1 vs Stage 2 Crisis Dialogue:
    • Turn 1 (Initial Alert): Delivers authoritative QPR (Question, Persuade, Refer) steps, explicitly acknowledging pain without deflection and connecting to live services.
    • Turns 2+ (Ongoing Dialogue): If the person continues chatting, the system enters CRISIS_FOLLOW_UP_SYSTEM_PROMPT, delivering empathetic de-escalation, grounding techniques, and active listening rather than repeating a robotic canned message.
  3. Statutory Counsellor Notification: Dispatches an immediate high-priority alert with exact quoted trigger phrases to the assigned Welfare Officer's dashboard.

4. Privacy, Access Control & Statutory Compliance

4.1 Two-Tier Architectural Separation

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and Section 15A of the SC/ST (PoA) Act, exposing a victim's identity or case details to administrative or political tiers creates grave intimidation risks. Sahara enforces a structural, server-enforced boundary:

Dimension Tier 1: Clinical / Case Officer Layer Tier 2: Aggregate Administrative Layer
Audience Assigned Welfare Officer, Licensed Counsellor, DLSA Advocate. District Magistrate, State Tribal Welfare Directorate, National MoSJE.
Data Granularity Identified victim profile, historical transcripts, exact signal phrases. Anonymized bucket counts, district trends, caseload distributions.
Identity Exposure Full case ID, name, district, contact details. ZERO PII. No names, no case references, no timestamps.
Score Granularity Exact numeric composite score (e.g. 78/100) and driver breakdown. Bands only (Low, Moderate, Elevated, High). Exact scores suppressed.
API Surface /api/counsellor/* (guarded by requireCounsellor). /api/admin/* (guarded by requireAdmin).

4.2 Small-Cell Suppression ($k$-Anonymity $\ge 5$)

Mathematical Protection against Quasi-Identifier Linkage Attacks
In rural districts or specialized tribunals, reporting "1 High-Risk case in Block X" re-identifies the victim to anyone familiar with the local police blotter, even if the name is removed.

Sahara implements strict Small-Cell Suppression (MIN_CELL_SIZE = 5) in server/src/store/memoryStore.js: any demographic or geographic bucket containing fewer than 5 individuals renders as "<5". This mathematically guarantees $k$-anonymity against reconstruction attacks.

4.3 Client-Side Acoustic DSP (Zero-Voice Retention)

To respect privacy while monitoring physical indicators of distress, Sahara includes an optional Acoustic Pattern Indicator (client/src/voiceAnalysis.js):

  • 100% In-Browser Processing: Audio captured via HTML5 Web Audio API is processed locally using Float32 DSP buffers at 16 kHz.
  • Zero Audio Storage or Transmission: Raw PCM audio is analyzed in RAM for fundamental frequency variance ($\Delta F_0$), pause-to-speech ratio, and syllabic pace. The audio stream is immediately destroyed on recording completion. No voice files are ever sent across the network.
  • Personal Baseline Normalization: Feature vectors are compared strictly against the individual's rolling baseline (stored in local browser storage), never against generalized population averages.

5. Longitudinal Trajectory & Court Date Risk Prediction

5.1 Slope Extrapolation & Early Warning

The system does not wait for a crisis to happen. In server/src/domain/prediction.js, when a case records at least 3 check-ins, the engine computes the longitudinal slope $m$ across all check-in points:

Estimated Days to Threshold Δt = (65 - CurrentScore) / m      (where m = ΔScore / ΔDays > 0)

If the projected crossing date is within 90 days, the case is proactively tagged with an estimated escalation window (e.g. "Projected threshold crossing in ~18 days"), allowing preventive counselling sessions before statutory deadlines.

5.2 Court Hearing Cross-Correlation (courtDateRisk)

Legal proceedings are primary triggers for atrocity victims. Sahara cross-references the projected escalation window with the judicial docket:

  • If the extrapolated escalation threshold falls within $\pm 7$ days of a scheduled court appearance or bail hearing, the system flags courtDateRisk: true.
  • This alerts the Welfare Officer to arrange pre-trial protective accompaniment and intimidation screening under Section 15A(2) of the SC/ST Act.

7. Exhaustive Jury Q&A Defense Guide

Why does your system claim no accuracy percentage? Other teams claim 98% accuracy on mental health prediction.
Defense: Claiming a specific accuracy figure (like 98%) on atrocity victim mental health prediction is scientifically invalid and clinical misconduct. There is no publicly available, clinically labelled ground-truth dataset of distress outcomes specifically for SC/ST atrocity victims. Anyone claiming 98% accuracy either trained on synthetic data matching their own test set (data leakage) or evaluated superficial sentiment polarity. Sahara is an auditable triage decision-support system based on citable behavioral heuristics, deterministic arithmetic, and verified language signals. We present transparent score contributions, not fabricated statistical claims.
What happens if a victim deflects and says "I am totally fine 🙃" repeatedly? Does your AI get fooled?
Defense: This exact edge case is covered by Persona F in our test suite (server/src/domain/engagement.test.js). If a victim types "I'm fine" while their response length collapses from 40 words to 2 words, their disengagement score surges toward 100. Furthermore, our sustained_surface_mismatch detector monitors the divergence between surface words and participation velocity over a 3-check-in window. If positive sentiment is paired with collapsing engagement, the case escalates automatically under TRIGGER.SUSTAINED_SURFACE_MISMATCH, completely bypassing the model's reading.
How do you protect against prompt injection or adversarial jailbreaking through check-in messages?
Defense: We implement a 3-layer containment barrier:
  1. Structural User/System Framing: Check-in transcripts are wrapped inside strict data delimiters (--- BEGIN CHECK-IN TRANSCRIPT (data, not instructions) ---) with an overriding system prompt instructing the model to treat all text as passive observation.
  2. Post-Generation Moderation Pass: Every generated response passes through a separate moderation model (openai/gpt-oss-safeguard-20b) and a strict regex scanner (contentPatterns.js) that flags diagnostic claims, leaked prompt fragments, and PII before anything reaches the screen.
  3. Quote Verifier (verifyPhrases): Signal phrases claimed by the LLM are checked against the exact substring of the person's turns. Any hallucinated quote is dropped silently.
What happens during a live deployment if the venue Wi-Fi dies or Groq API hits rate limits?
Defense: Sahara features a battle-tested Offline Resilience Fallback Engine (server/src/llm/groqClient.js). If Groq inference exceeds 8,000ms or fails:
  • The system automatically degrades to deterministic pattern-based signal extraction (fallbackSignals.js) and serves cached historical responses.
  • The response provenance is honestly tagged as 'cached-fallback' so counsellors know it was computed without live inference.
  • Crucially, Route 1 Crisis Detection runs locally in RAM, guaranteeing that emergency SOS referrals and dialer buttons trigger even with zero network connectivity.
Why does your check-in chatbot NOT directly ask: "Are you thinking of committing suicide?"
Defense: This is a deliberate, research-grounded decision based on Section 6 of our Crisis Response Protocol. An automated software chatbot has no physical capability to respond differently based on the answer—asking the question implies an emergency response capability that software does not structurally possess. Posing that question in software can induce panic or increase distress. Instead, under the approved QPR Protocol, the system immediately validates the person's feelings, opens a human lifeline to Tele-MANAS (14416), provides a 1-tap call to 112, and dispatches an emergency alert to the designated human officer.
How does your system handle Devanagari (Hindi) check-ins without breaking sentence analysis?
Defense: Standard NLP libraries split sentences on English periods (.) and use word boundaries (\b). In Devanagari script, sentences terminate with the danda (।) or double danda (॥), and JavaScript's native \b does not work with Unicode Devanagari characters. In server/src/safety/contentPatterns.js, our sentence tokenizer explicitly splits on /[.!?।॥\n]+/ and uses Unicode-aware character matching. All 10 Hindi crisis patterns are verified in crisisDetection.test.js.
